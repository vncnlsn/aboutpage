About

APS Land Services

APS specializes in the preparation and field coordination our American energy and infrastructure clients depend on. Before surveying and exploration work begins, we evaluate geography, topography, and regulatory requirements, research land and mineral ownership, and secure the permits and agreements required for field access. Once crews begin work, we remain actively involved, ensuring operations follow project plans, safety requirements, and contractual agreements. Mark Fischbuch founded Allstate Permit Services on that responsibility in 1980, and it still defines our focus today.

Our work has spanned 25 states and territories, hundreds of successful projects, and thousands of square miles successfully permitted. We don’t outsource communication; our team has coordinated with thousands of landowners across private, state, federal, and tribal lands. Decades of experience have refined our processes, technology, and project management capabilities without losing sight of what matters most: getting the details right. We take great pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects we’re entrusted with.

In August 2026, we became APS Land Services. The new name reflects the depth of expertise and range of capabilities we’ve built through decades in the field while carrying forward the standard we began with: hard work, honesty, and being exceptionally good at what we do.

For Landowners

You Stay in Control of What Happens on Your Land

If an APS agent has reached out, it is because a company is exploring whether your property may be part of an upcoming survey project. APS works on behalf of energy and infrastructure companies to secure temporary, compensated access for survey work. The agreement defines what access is permitted, where work may occur, and the terms that apply.

This is not:

  • A lease
  • A transfer of mineral rights
  • Drilling
  • Permanent access
01

Limited Access

Permission is for a defined purpose and period of time. You retain ownership and control of your property.

02

You Are Compensated

Landowners are compensated for the access and activities agreed to before work begins.

03

Mapping What Lies Below

Seismic surveys help map the geology and potential resources beneath the surface.

04

APS Stays Involved

We coordinate with landowners, clients, and field crews to help ensure work follows the terms of the agreement.

We’re here to answer questions at any point — who we’re representing, why we’ve contacted you, what the proposed work involves, and what your agreement entails.
