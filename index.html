About Our Company

Built on integrity, relationships, and execution.

For over four decades, APS Land Services has provided trusted land acquisition, permitting, and field coordination for American energy and infrastructure projects.

Our Legacy

Four decades in the making.

Founded in 1980 as Allstate Permit Services, our company was built on a straightforward premise: energy and infrastructure projects succeed only when land rights and permits are handled with total accuracy and absolute transparency. What began as a regional permitting operation quickly grew as clients recognized the value of a team that kept its word, met its deadlines, and treated property owners with respect.

Over the years, as energy development expanded and regulatory environments became increasingly complex, we evolved alongside the industry. Rebranding to APS Land Services reflects our comprehensive capabilities—expanding from specialized seismic permitting into complete right-of-way acquisition, advanced GIS mapping, and thorough title curative services across 23 states and more than 100 counties.

Today, while our reach and technology have expanded significantly, our core operating philosophy remains unchanged. We still believe in direct manager oversight, turnkey pricing without surprise fees, and personal accountability. Every permit filed and every agreement negotiated carries four decades of reputation behind it.
